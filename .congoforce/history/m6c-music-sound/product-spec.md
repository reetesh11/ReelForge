# Product spec — Add music, sound effects, ambience and silence cues to the reel mix

## User-facing behaviour

The owner can add a scene-tied list of audio cues to a story. Each cue is generated locally or taken from the owner's own file. The cues are mixed into the reel with ducking and designed silence. Every cue is licence-tracked. Decision from the owner (answers.md): this is one task, the measurement spike (R15) is the first implementation step, and if no model reaches the music tracks, music uses the owner's own files while generation stays for effects and ambience.

### Cue list in `story.yaml`
- A story may have an ordered `audio` list. Each cue has:
  - `id`: unique within the story.
  - `kind`: one of `music`, `sfx`, `ambience`, `silence`.
  - `start`: a scene id plus an offset in seconds (for example `s3` with offset 1.5), or `scene_start` or `scene_end` of a scene.
  - `end` or `duration`: one of the two. The resolved length is `end - start`.
  - `prompt`: text for generation.
  - `source`: `generate` or `file`.
  - Optional `gain_db`, `fade_in_s`, `fade_out_s`.
  - `duck`: true or false, music only.
- A `silence` cue has no prompt and no source; both are ignored if given.
- A cue with `source: file` has an uploaded clip. Its `prompt` is optional and is a note only.
- Cues from the structuring step carry state `suggested` until the owner approves them. Other states are `draft` (no take yet), `ready` (has a picked take), `stale` (needs update).
- When `audio` is present the old `music` block is ignored (kept in the file, not shown, not mixed). A story with only `music` loads as before and the Sound screen shows it as empty with a hint: "This story uses the old music note. Add cues to design the sound."

### Validation messages
Rejected cues give a message of the form `Cue "<id>": <problem>`:
- Unknown scene: `Cue "gong1": unknown scene "s99".`
- Negative time: `Cue "gong1": start must not be negative.` (same for end or duration).
- End before start: `Cue "gong1": end (2.0 s) is before start (3.0 s).`
- Unknown kind: `Cue "gong1": unknown kind "boom"; use music, sfx, ambience or silence.`
- Duplicate id: `Cue "gong1": id is used more than once.`
- Both `end` and `duration` given, or neither: `Cue "gong1": give either end or duration.` (Implied by R1.)
- A zero-length cue is rejected as end not after start.

### Generation and takes
- A generate cue is generated by an explicit "Generate" or "Re-take" action on the Sound screen (and the matching API call). Nothing generates automatically.
- Each take is exactly the cue length (mock backend: within 50 ms). Takes are versioned `take 1`, `take 2`, and so on. Earlier takes are kept and can be played or picked. Each take records prompt, duration, seed and model.
- Music longer than the model limit is made by generating up to the limit and extending or looping with a crossfade. The owner sees one take of the full length.
- Progress and failure use the same job states and copy as voice and shot jobs ("Queued", "Running", "Failed", with the existing Retry).

### Own files
- On a `source: file` cue the owner uploads a clip. It is checked by content and size, as voice reference clips are. A non-audio file is refused with the same style of message as voice references: `That file is not an audio clip.` An over-size file is refused with the existing size message.
- A clip shorter than the cue is looped; a longer one is trimmed. The result is exactly the cue length.
- At upload the owner fills a licence note (free text). Without it the upload is accepted and flagged "No licence note" in the cue row and in the export licence check.

### Mix
- The EDL carries each cue with absolute start and end in seconds, gain and fades. Dialogue, music, sfx and ambience are mixed with per-cue fades.
- Ducking: while dialogue speaks, music cues with `duck: true` drop by the ducking amount (default 9 dB), attack 150 ms, release 400 ms. The ducking amount is editable per project on the Sound screen (field "Duck amount (dB)"). Attack, release and silence fade are stored per project with these defaults. They are not shown on the screen unless the owner needs them. See Open questions.
- Silence: a `silence` cue forces all music, sfx and ambience to zero in its span with fades (default 80 ms). Dialogue is unaffected.
- Final loudness stays at about -14 LUFS via the existing two-pass loudnorm.
- A cue with no picked take is skipped in the mix, and the preview and export panels say how many: "2 cues have no audio and are left out of the mix."

### Sound screen
- It is a step after Shots and before Assembly in the project flow, with Back and Next following the existing screens (Assembly's Back now goes to Sound).
- Layout: a timeline grouped per scene. Each cue is a row showing order number, kind, label (id), source (Generate or File), resolved start and duration in seconds, state badge, and model licence (for example "Apache-2.0", or "Your file: <licence note>", or "No licence note").
- Row actions: edit, delete (with confirmation), move up, move down, play the picked take, Re-take, pick a take (list of takes with play and "Use this take"), upload file (file cues).
- Add cue: a form with the fields above. Scene and offset are chosen from the scene list.
- "Preview full mix": renders a preview of the whole mix from the picked takes and plays it. It is not an export and creates no `vNNN` folder.
- Suggested cues show a "Suggested" badge, with Approve and Dismiss. Approve makes the cue a draft. Generate stays unavailable until it is approved.
- Empty state: "No sound cues yet. Add a cue or let the structuring step suggest some from your storyline."
- If the engine is not available, generate actions are disabled with the same hint other screens use.
- When the cues change, the cue gets the badge "Needs update". The Assembly screen shows its existing stale prompt. The owner chooses when to re-take.

### Seeding from the storyline
- When structuring finds music or sound directions in the storyline text (for example "TRACK 2 kicks in", "gong slam", "complete silence"), it adds cues with state `suggested`, scene-tied where the text says so. No job starts.
- Re-running structuring does not duplicate suggested cues already present, and never deletes or changes cues the owner approved or edited.

### Credits and licence check
- `credits.json` lists every generated or uploaded cue with: cue id, kind, model, version, licence. Uploads list the file name and the owner-supplied licence note in place of model and version.
- The export licence check (the existing "Licence check before export" dialog) also lists non-commercial models and uploads without a licence note. Upload lines read: `<file name> - Licence note not filled in`. The existing "Export anyway" and "Cancel" actions are unchanged.
- Only commercially-friendly licences (MIT, Apache-2.0 or similar) are allowed for audio models in `models.toml`. A model with another licence is not allowed in.

### Staleness and versions
- Changing a cue's prompt, timing, kind or audio file marks only that cue's take stale, and the assembly stale. No job starts.
- Changing a cue's gain, fades or duck changes the mix only. It marks the assembly stale but not the take. (Implied by R9, since those fields do not alter the take audio.)
- Earlier reel exports (`v001`, `v002`) are never changed or removed.

### Spike and manual checks
- `tools/` gains a script that, on the reference Mac, generates the three music descriptions, the screenplay effects and a wedding ambience at 3 durations each per candidate model, and writes a table: generation time, peak memory, loudness, duration accuracy and a note per file. The owner's per-file verdicts, the chosen model per cue kind and the default prompts go in `docs/audio-levers.md`. Models that fail licence or Mac support are dropped before listening.
- Cue lengths: the three music tracks use the owner's descriptions (shehnai and dholak festive; tabla and brass tension; sarangi and flute melody). Effects: drum roll, gong slam, ceramic crash and splash, packaging crinkle, crunch.
- The reference case is the ten Gujiya cues listed in `input.md`.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC-1: A story with an `audio` list validates. Unknown scene, negative time, end before start and unknown kind are each rejected with `Cue "<id>": <problem>` naming the cue and the problem (copy above). A story with only the old `music` block still loads. A story with both loads, and only `audio` is used.
- AC-2: Generating a music cue and an sfx cue on the mock backend gives takes whose length is within 50 ms of the cue length. Each take records prompt, duration, seed and model. A second take is `take 2`, the first stays listed and playable.
- AC-3: Uploading a non-audio file as a cue file is rejected by content check, with `That file is not an audio clip.` and no take is created. A valid clip shorter than the cue is looped, and a longer one trimmed, to the exact cue length (within 50 ms).
- AC-4: In an exported test reel with known tones, the level in each music, sfx and ambience cue's window is above the level just outside it, and each cue starts within 50 ms of its resolved time. The mix is made without a shell (argument list).
- AC-5: With `duck: true`, music level during a dialogue line is lower than before and after it by the configured amount (default 9 dB) within 1.5 dB, with attack 150 ms and release 400 ms applied. With `duck: false` the level is unchanged. Changing the project ducking amount changes the drop to match.
- AC-6: In a `silence` cue's span, music, sfx and ambience measure below -60 dBFS after the fades (default 80 ms), while dialogue still plays. Outside the span they are audible.
- AC-7: Editing a cue's prompt marks only that cue's take and the assembly stale, and starts no job. Unchanged cues are not stale. The earlier reel version still exists.
- AC-8: The Sound screen lists cues in order with source, duration and licence. Add, edit, delete, reorder, play, re-take, pick a take and upload work (vitest, mock backend). The screen sits between Shots and Assembly. Empty state copy as above.
- AC-9: Suggested cues from structuring are marked `suggested`. No generation job starts until a cue is approved, including by the API (refused with `Approve this cue before generating it.`).
- AC-10: `credits.json` lists every cue with model, version and licence. A non-commercial model, or an upload with no licence note, triggers the export licence check dialog and lists the offender.
- AC-11: The full path (cues, generation, mix, export) completes on the mock backend with no outbound network connection.
- AC-12: Existing frozen M1 to M6b tests pass unchanged. New response fields are omitted when empty.
- AC-13: (Manual) The spike has been run on the reference Mac. `docs/audio-levers.md` holds its table, the owner's per-file verdicts, the chosen model per cue kind and the default prompts. Models failing licence or Mac support are dropped before listening. If no music model reaches the character, the file records that music uses the owner's own files.
- AC-14: (Manual) The owner re-exports the Gujiya reel with the ten reference cues, listens, and records in `docs/manual-checks.md` whether levels, ducking and silence are acceptable.
- AC-15: (Implied by R3, R9) With `audio` present, cues that are unchanged keep their takes when the story is saved again; saving without any cue change marks nothing stale.
- AC-16: (Implied by R14) The preview mix and export both use the mock tones on the mock backend, and a final loudness of about -14 LUFS (within the existing loudnorm tolerance) holds for a reel with cues.

## Edge cases and errors
- Cue extends past the end of the reel: allowed in the story; the mix is cut at the reel end, and the Sound screen shows a note "Ends after the reel."
- Cue refers to a scene that is later deleted: the cue is flagged "Scene missing" and blocks the mix with the validation message naming the cue.
- Overlapping cues of the same kind are allowed and mixed together.
- A silence cue inside a music cue silences only its span; music resumes after the fade.
- A scene change that moves times moves the cue and marks its take stale if the length changed.
- Upload: empty file, wrong type or too large is refused with no change to the cue. Upload with no cue length (invalid cue) is refused.
- A failed generation leaves the earlier take picked, with the existing failed job state and Retry.
- Duplicate click on Re-take while a job is running does not start a second job.
- A generate cue with an empty prompt is refused: `Cue "<id>": prompt is empty.`
- An audio model licence not commercially-friendly in `models.toml` is rejected at load; a missing model shows in the Settings status page as with other models.
- No outbound connection is made at render time; a generation that needs a download not yet done fails with the existing "model not installed" message.

## Out of scope
- Screenplay import and product-image compositing (next).
- Voice changes.
- Stem separation, music editing tools, beat-matching to cuts.
- Cloud music or sound services.
- M7 hardening.

## Open questions
- Should ducking attack, release and the silence fade be editable on the Sound screen, or only the ducking amount (req.md says "editable per project" for the amount only)? This spec assumes amount only on screen, others stored with defaults.
- Should "Approve" on a suggested cue be a single click per cue, or is an "Approve all" also wanted?
- Should changing only gain, fades or duck mark the assembly stale without marking the take stale (assumed here)?
- Where the final licence text for each model shows when the spike picks a model: the model's `models.toml` licence field is assumed to be the label shown.
